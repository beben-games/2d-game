## Decided at the plan (from the code survey, 2026-10-04)

These settle what the design left to the code; each is mirrored in the design's deviations.

1. **The view has a margin.** Tier 1's arena is 448 px wide and the view 426.67, so the camera scrolls about 21 px sideways and the shake moves it further. "On screen" is inside `View.rect(node)`: the visible world rect grown by `View.MARGIN` (16 px, a tile). With it the tier 1 walls are always inside the edge, so the rules and the arrows change nothing there.
2. **Tier 2 for a save with a win is computed, not migrated.** `Save.highest_tier()` is the larger of the stored unlock and what the wins imply (a win with no stored unlock means 2), so an older file, a wiped one, and an act preset all agree.
3. **The tier survives a restart.** The chosen tier lives in `RunState.tier` (an autoload outlives the scene reload); `Main` reads its series from it.
4. **A boss of several bodies.** The spawner's seat, the HUD's bar, the favour paid by damage, the piles, the fight clock, the profile's `boss_kills`, and the summons' death each assume one body today; Task 8 makes each count the bodies.
5. **"The handler drives the beast"** is built as the beast's stage: with the handler alive it is in its first stage; the handler's death enrages it (the existing `request_enrage`: its second-stage timings and cycle). No separate driving mechanic.
6. **The boss bars sit at the screen's top**, side by side when there are two (in tier 2 that row is no longer the wall's ledge).
