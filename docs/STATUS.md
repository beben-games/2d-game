## How to run and verify

```bash
tools/run.sh                                                # play: WASD, mouse aim, click to shoot, Space/right click dash, 1/2/3 or click picks a card, Tab or Esc pauses (build, volumes, restart, quit), R restarts
tools/run.sh --seed=N                                       # replay a run, skipping the title: same waves, and the same card offers given the same picks and the same hurt state at each clear (the seed is on the summary and in the RUN_OVER/RUN_WON line)
tools/test.sh                                               # all suites headless; exit 0 pass
tools/check_boot.sh                                         # boots main scene headless, fails on any error
tools/smoke.sh <idle|move|combat|kill|room|death|pick|title|pause|boss>   # scripted run in a 1280x720 window, screenshot in reports/; a 30 s in-process watchdog, reported as `watchdog: scenario hung`; every scenario fails if it ran silent (SMOKE_AUDIO 0)
tools/build.sh                                              # tester zips for Windows x64 and Linux x64 into builds/ (needs the export templates)
tools/input_probe.sh 25                                     # logs raw key/mouse/focus events (stuck-key diagnosis)
tools/gen_icons.py                                          # regenerates data/icons.json from the (row, col) table inside it
source tools/godot.sh && perl -e 'alarm 120; exec @ARGV' "$GODOT_BIN" --headless --path . -s tools/icon_sheet.gd </dev/null   # renders every icon to reports/icons.png; gen_guns.gd runs the same way
```

Smoke scenarios: `idle` (the first room, nothing spawning), `move` (run right and dash), `combat` (the real floor, the first wave under fire), `kill` (a placed chaser dies, `SMOKE_KILLS 1`), `room` (clear a one-chaser room, take card 1, walk through the door, `SMOKE_ROOM 1`), `death` (hp 1 against a chaser, `SMOKE_SUMMARY You died`), `pick` (clear the room, capture the paused cards as `reports/smoke_pick_menu.png`, take card 1, `SMOKE_UPGRADE <id>`). `room`, `death`, and `pick` run on `tools/smoke_floor.tres`; `title` (the front door, `smoke_title_screen.png`, then Play), `pause` (Esc, `smoke_pause_menu.png`, Esc), `boss` (`tools/smoke_boss_floor.tres`, shoots the boss until its first ring, `SMOKE_BOSS_HP n of 60` below max under a visible bar).

Testing aids: the title's seed field takes cheat codes from `scripts/cheats.gd` (`permawhat?` makes the player immortal); a cheated run takes a random seed and its summary and `RUN_OVER`/`RUN_WON` line say `cheats=<flags>`.

Tuning numbers: `data/enemies/*.tres` (shooter range and telegraph, chaser speed, the boss's timings and attacks in `boss.tres`), `data/weapons/*.tres` (handgun, crossbow, shaman bolt), `data/upgrades/*.tres` (every card's modifiers per rank and its `max_rank`), `data/waves/*.tres` (breathers, groups), `data/rooms/*.tres` (size, exit), `data/audio.json` (each sound's file, volume, pitch jitter, minimum gap; the loops' levels), `scripts/dash_rules.gd` (speed, duration, the per-charge cooldown), `scripts/build.gd` (`BASE_MAX_HP`, `BASE_DASH_CHARGES`), `scripts/heart_rules.gd` (the heal amount), `scripts/status_effects.gd` (burn ticks and damage, stun and chill times, the tints), `scripts/projectile.gd` (homing range and turn, the wall nudge, `BOLT_SCALE`, the trail constants), `scripts/main.gd` (`FADE_TIME`, `ENTRY_SEAL_DELAY`, summary delays), `scripts/autoload/juice.gd`, `scripts/fx.gd` (the particle recipes), the trauma and hitstop consts at the top of `scripts/enemy.gd`, `scripts/boss.gd`, and `scripts/player.gd`, `scripts/camera.gd`, the card and panel sizes at the top of `scripts/ui/upgrade_menu.gd` and `scripts/ui/build_screen.gd`, the HUD scales and the boss bar in `scripts/ui/hud.gd`, `scripts/settings.gd` (the default volumes). `docs/plans/2026-09-22-m4-feel-checklist.md` maps each playtest question to its number.
