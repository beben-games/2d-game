## What exists

A playable run on `main`: eight single-screen rooms with an upgrade card after each of the first seven and an ending. Milestone 3 is a candidate at tag `m3-candidate` (2026-09-14), awaiting the user's playtest; Milestone 2 closed at tag `m2` on 2026-09-07; Milestone 1 at `m1`. Godot 4.7.2, GDScript, 278 gdUnit4 tests in 41 suites green, boot gate and seven smoke scenarios green.

- Milestone 0 (tag `m0`): project scaffold, headless test runner and boot gate, screenshot smoke tool, 0x72 Dungeon Tileset II with a name-based `SpriteAtlas`, `Events` and `RunState` autoloads, tiled arena with wall colliders, animated player movement, following camera with aim lean.
- Milestone 1 (tag `m1`): pistol (`WeaponDef`, `FireController`, `Projectile`), `Health` component, imp Chaser enemy with a spawn fade and chase state, `Juice` autoload (trauma shake, extending hitstop, hit flash), particles and muzzle flash, player HP with contact damage, i-frame blink, knockback, death, and restart. Solid enemy bodies, 3x zoom, fullscreen, and death holding until R came from the playtests.
- Milestone 2 (tag `m2`; the door pass and replay 2 fixes landed after the tag): single-screen 28x15 rooms (`Room` scene: arena, doors, enemies, projectiles, spawner, wave runner) built from `RoomDef`s, ringed by a two-row top wall (the tileset's ledge over its brick face, with the exit door set into the band) and one-row side and bottom walls (the entry is a dark opening with shaded wall ends that bricks up 0.4 s after you arrive), leaving a 26x12 floor; a floor of authored rooms (`data/floors/floor_1.tres`, four rooms then, eight now) with authored waves (`data/waves/room_N.tres`, `WaveTable` > `WaveDef` > `SpawnGroup`) driven by `WaveRunner` and the pure `WaveProgress`; chasers run at the player's 110 px/s so the dash is the escape; the Shooter enemy (orc shaman, `ShooterBrain`) that keeps its range and fires a telegraphed 120 px/s bolt; a dash on Space or right click (`DashRules`, layer 7 so triggers still see a dashing player); a HUD with tileset hearts, room, wave, and kills; doors that open on clear with a real-time fade between rooms; a run summary on death ("You died") and on the last clear ("Floor cleared") that waits for R; `-- --seed=N` replays a run. The heart pickup that dropped on every clear was removed in Milestone 3 (Heal is a card now). The `Spawner` is a placement service (`spawn(scene, at)`), scene tests share `tests/support/scene_suite.gd`, and the smoke tool gained `kill`, `room`, and `death` scenarios with a 30 s watchdog.
- Milestone 3 (candidate, tag `m3-candidate`; design `docs/plans/2026-09-08-milestone-3-design.md`, build record `docs/plans/2026-09-08-milestone-3.md`): the room-clear moment is a paused three-card upgrade menu (`UpgradeMenu`, CanvasLayer 10, 0x72 frame and pixel font, Raven icons; keys 1/2/3 or a click, R restarts) on every clear but the last. The run holds a `Build` (weapon id plus upgrade ranks) that a pure fold resolves into the `WeaponDef` the player fires; nothing mutates weapon data. `UpgradeDef` files in `data/upgrades/` (15 rank cards, Heal, and a Switch card per other weapon) list `Modifier`s per rank; `UpgradeCatalog` loads and validates them and computes the pool (this weapon's cards below cap, player cards, Heal when hurt, the switch) and a seeded draw (`RunState.stream("upgrades:room:round")`). The crossbow is the second weapon (bolts drawn from the tileset's `weapon_arrow`, slow, heavy, pierce 2); a switch is one pick and re-offers as many rounds as the old weapon had upgrades. Projectiles move by raycast so they never tunnel and can bounce (`bounce`) and turn toward enemies (`homing`); burn, stun, and chill live in a `StatusEffects` child on every enemy (a stun interrupts a shooter's telegraph); the flash shader now lets `modulate` through, so the tints and the spawn fade-in are visible. Dash charges (`DashRules` refill one at a time), heart containers up to six hearts, the HUD build strip with dash pips, a Tab build screen (`BuildScreen`), and rooms 5 to 8 (`data/rooms`, `data/waves`, all exits TOP, counts rising to a room 8 finale). Assets: Raven Fantasy Icons (`assets/raven_icons`, `data/icons.json` via `tools/gen_icons.py`), the 0x72 dungeon UI sheet and its font (`assets/dungeon_ui`, `tools/gen_ui_font.gd`), the user's pistol sheet cut to a handgun (`assets/guns`, `tools/gen_guns.gd`), reserve packs in `assets/reserve`, all in `CREDITS.md`.
- Design decisions taken during review, all recorded in the plans: `room_cleared` and `enemy_died` arrive from physics callbacks, so the exit request and the picker's open are deferred; the first ending (win or death) claims the run and later events change nothing; a death during the fade holds on the corpse under the black; the summary draws over the fade (CanvasLayer 30 over 20 over the menus at 10 over the HUD at 1); `quiet_main_with_floor` is quiet for the first room only; refund rounds accumulate across a switch taken during a refund round; corpses take no status and are never a homing target; `Main.restart()` closes the menu and the build screen so no live overlay sits over a test's game.

## How to run and verify

```bash
source tools/godot.sh && "$GODOT_BIN" --path .              # play: WASD, mouse aim, click to shoot, Space/right click dash, 1/2/3 or click picks a card, Tab shows the build, R restarts
source tools/godot.sh && "$GODOT_BIN" --path . -- --seed=N  # replay a run: same waves, and the same card offers given the same picks and the same hurt state at each clear (the seed is on the summary and in the RUN_OVER/RUN_WON line)
tools/test.sh                                               # all suites headless; exit 0 pass
tools/check_boot.sh                                         # boots main scene headless, fails on any error
tools/smoke.sh <idle|move|combat|kill|room|death|pick>      # scripted run in a 1280x720 window, screenshot in reports/; a 30 s in-process watchdog, reported as `watchdog: scenario hung`
tools/input_probe.sh 25                                     # logs raw key/mouse/focus events (stuck-key diagnosis)
tools/gen_icons.py                                          # regenerates data/icons.json from the (row, col) table inside it
source tools/godot.sh && perl -e 'alarm 120; exec @ARGV' "$GODOT_BIN" --headless --path . -s tools/gen_ui_font.gd </dev/null   # the BMFont from the 0x72 sheet; gen_guns.gd and icon_sheet.gd run the same way
```

Smoke scenarios: `idle` (the first room, nothing spawning), `move` (run right and dash), `combat` (the real floor, the first wave under fire), `kill` (a placed chaser dies, `SMOKE_KILLS 1`), `room` (clear a one-chaser room, take card 1, walk through the door, `SMOKE_ROOM 1`), `death` (hp 1 against a chaser, `SMOKE_SUMMARY You died`), `pick` (clear the room, capture the paused cards as `reports/smoke_pick_menu.png`, take card 1, `SMOKE_UPGRADE <id>`). `room`, `death`, and `pick` run on `tools/smoke_floor.tres`.

Tuning numbers: `data/enemies/*.tres` (shooter range and telegraph, chaser speed), `data/weapons/*.tres` (handgun, crossbow, shaman bolt), `data/upgrades/*.tres` (every card's modifiers per rank and its `max_rank`), `data/waves/*.tres` (breathers, groups), `data/rooms/*.tres` (size, exit), `scripts/dash_rules.gd` (speed, duration, the per-charge cooldown), `scripts/build.gd` (`BASE_MAX_HP`, `BASE_DASH_CHARGES`), `scripts/status_effects.gd` (burn ticks and damage, stun and chill times, the tints), `scripts/projectile.gd` (homing range and turn, the wall nudge), `scripts/main.gd` (`FADE_TIME`, `ENTRY_SEAL_DELAY`, summary delays), `scripts/autoload/juice.gd`, the trauma and hitstop consts at the top of `scripts/enemy.gd` and `scripts/player.gd`, `scripts/camera.gd`, the card and panel sizes at the top of `scripts/ui/upgrade_menu.gd` and `scripts/ui/build_screen.gd`, the HUD scales in `scripts/ui/hud.gd`. `docs/plans/2026-09-14-m3-feel-checklist.md` maps each playtest question to its number.

## How the work was done

Subagent-driven development from the plans in `docs/plans/2026-09-02-arena-roguelike-m0-m1.md`, `docs/plans/2026-09-04-milestone-2.md`, and `docs/plans/2026-09-08-milestone-3.md`: one implementer per task, then a spec-compliance review, then a code-quality review, with fixes re-reviewed before the next task, and the plan re-synced with every deviation. Milestone 3 used the same loop over fifteen tasks; its plan records every deviation found in review (the flash shader that ignored `modulate`, the load cycle through the enemy bolt, the refund rounds that accumulate, the stun that interrupts a telegraph, and the rest). The plans are the record of what was built and why; they are long because they hold the final code of every file. `CLAUDE.md` holds the conventions a session needs to keep working the same way.
