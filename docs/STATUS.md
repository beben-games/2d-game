## Public repository

The project is public at https://github.com/beben-games/2d-game (set up 2026-09-24): code MIT (`LICENSE`), the asset licenses and the Godot notice in `CREDITS.md`, a public `README.md`. The packs whose terms forbid reposting (Raven icons, the pistol sheet, the Minifantasy and Classic Monster effects, the M5 crowd, coin, and verdict sounds, the three music loops, the reserve sets) are gitignored and were removed from the whole history before the first push, and the author email became the GitHub noreply address, so every commit SHA changed then (the pre-rewrite repo is kept locally at `~/Claude/2d-game-backup-before-public.git`); `docs/commit-map.txt` maps the old SHAs cited in the plans to the new ones. Those files stay in the working copy and ship in the builds; `docs/ASSETS.md` says where to get them, and a clone without them draws placeholder icons (`ICON_MISSING`) and plays silence. Releases: `tools/build.sh` then `tools/release.sh` (GitHub release `v<version>` with both zips, which carry `LICENSE` and `CREDITS.md`; a `-rc` version is a pre-release). The latest is `v0.5.0-rc3`; `0.6.0-rc1` is the version on `main`, not yet built or released.

## How to run and verify

```bash
tools/run.sh                                                # play: WASD, mouse aim, click to shoot, Space/right click dash, E on what is in reach in the grounds (a door, the post, the rack, the lift, a character; in the box E, Enter, or a click goes on, 1 to 5 or a click picks a reply), 1 to 5 or click picks a card, Esc pauses on the Options tab (resume, restart, volumes, quit) and Tab on Boons (the build), a third tab shows Training (the profile's lines), either key closes; R restarts (a yield mid-run; nothing in the grounds)
tools/run.sh --seed=N                                       # replay a run, skipping the title: same waves, and the same card offers given the same picks and the same hurt state at each clear (the seed is on the gate screen and in the RUN_END line)
tools/test.sh                                               # all suites headless at --fixed-fps 60 (seconds), prints a digest; --realtime paces it on the wall clock, -v streams everything (both before -a); its log is reports/test_<pid>.log, moved to reports/test.log at exit; exit 0 pass
tools/check_boot.sh                                         # boots main scene headless and runs the reload probe at fixed fps (Play, Restart, Play, a fall to the gate screen, the grounds, Quit to title, Play into the Ludus, its top door, the lift, Quit to title from the run), fails on any error
tools/smoke.sh <idle|move|combat|kill|round|fall|wake|pick|roar|boo|title|pause|boss|grounds|rooms|talk>   # scripted run in a 1280x720 window, screenshot in reports/; a 30 s in-process watchdog, reported as `watchdog: scenario hung`; every scenario fails if it ran silent (SMOKE_AUDIO 0)
tools/build.sh                                              # tester zips for Windows x64 and Linux x64 into builds/ (needs the export templates)
tools/release.sh                                            # publishes them as the GitHub release v<version> (gh auth login; the commit pushed first)
tools/input_probe.sh 25                                     # logs raw key/mouse/focus events (stuck-key diagnosis)
tools/gen_icons.py                                          # regenerates data/icons.json from the (row, col) table inside it
source tools/godot.sh && perl -e 'alarm 120; exec @ARGV' "$GODOT_BIN" --headless --path . -s tools/icon_sheet.gd </dev/null   # renders every icon to reports/icons.png; gen_guns.gd runs the same way
```

Smoke scenarios: `idle` (the arena, nothing spawning), `move` (run right and dash), `combat` (the real series, the first wave under fire), `kill` (a placed chaser dies, `SMOKE_KILLS 1`, the counter reads 1), `round` (clear a one-chaser round, take card 1, wait the gap, `SMOKE_ROUND 1`), `fall` (hp 1 against a chaser; `reports/smoke_fall_verdict.png` at the thumb with the narrator's window at the bottom, `SMOKE_VERDICT up`, `SMOKE_NARRATOR verdict_up`, then the gate screen, `SMOKE_GATE Porta Triumphalis`; the end capture is the screen over the black, dark by design), `wake` (`verso`, a fall, `SMOKE_GATE Porta Libitinaria`, the gate passed into the Spoliarium, `SMOKE_WAKE spoliarium prone=true`, `reports/smoke_wake_prone.png` with the narrator's line at the top, a move press, `SMOKE_ROSE true`), `pick` (clear the round, capture the paused cards with the crowd's line over "Pick a boon" as `reports/smoke_pick_menu.png`, `SMOKE_CROWD_LINE crowd.<name>`, take card 1, `SMOKE_UPGRADE <id>`), `roar` (the `pick` flow at full favour, `reports/smoke_roar_menu.png` after the crowd's gold card drops into the fourth slot, `SMOKE_ROAR 4`, `SMOKE_CROWD_ROARS 2`), `boo` (the `pick` flow at a Boo, `reports/smoke_boo_menu.png` with one card chained, `SMOKE_BOO_LOCKED <index>`). `round`, `fall`, `wake`, `pick`, `roar`, and `boo` run on `tools/smoke_series.tres`; `title` (the front door, `smoke_title_screen.png`, then Play), `pause` (Esc, `smoke_pause_menu.png` on the Options tab, Esc), `boss` (`tools/smoke_boss_series.tres`, shoots the boss until its first ring, `SMOKE_BOSS_HP n of 600` below max under a visible bar). The grounds' three play a returned scratch profile into the Ludus: `grounds` (the first arrival's line, `reports/smoke_arrival.png`, `SMOKE_ARRIVAL <pool>.<name> open=true`; the walk up to the post, `reports/smoke_grounds_key.png` with the key cap and the lanista's mark; E through the lanista's word, `SMOKE_GROUNDS_WORD lanista.<name>`, then `SMOKE_GROUNDS post` with the training panel up in the end capture), `rooms` (every door walked by real input, `reports/smoke_room_<id>.png` for each of the five rooms and `smoke_room_door_key.png`, `SMOKE_ROOMS ludus armamentarium sanitarium hypogeum spoliarium`), `talk` (the veteran: `smoke_talk_mark.png`, `smoke_talk_key.png`, `smoke_talk_box.png`, `smoke_talk_choices.png`, `SMOKE_TALK veteran.<name> open=false`). The story's checks match a pool and a shape, never an event's name. The smoke tool and the reload probe use their own scratch profiles (`user://smoke_profile_<pid>.cfg`, `user://probe_profile_<pid>.cfg`, per process and removed at the end), never the player's save.
