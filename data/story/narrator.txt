# The narrator's pool: one line at a time in the window, with no input: at the verdict, over the
# drum roll and under the thumb, and as the gladiator wakes in the Spoliarium after a thumbs down
# (an entry event, timed because the pool is: shown once the black lifts, for a few seconds).
# Every line is a PLACEHOLDER for the human writing. A timed event shows only its first shown line
# (a second line is never shown), so each event is one line. A verdict comes every run, so every
# event repeats; the lines of a moment rotate (the least recently played first). The verdict's
# lines are chosen before the run is banked: the profile's counts and the last_* names are the
# runs before this one, run_band is this run's. The wake's lines read the arrival: through the
# gate screen's pass into the Spoliarium is a thumbs down's wake (arrival == gate); a walk in
# through its door says nothing.

== weighing
trigger: verdict_wait
repeat

NARRATOR: PLACEHOLDER The emperor is deciding your fate.

== weighing_at_boo
trigger: verdict_wait
when: run_band == boo
repeat

NARRATOR: PLACEHOLDER The crowd has turned its back on you.
