# The veteran's pool: the old fighter in the Ludus's yard. Every line is a PLACEHOLDER for the
# human writing. The first word ends in a choice whose flag the next word reads.

== introduction
priority: high
act: 1

VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER New. You still look at the sand as if it might be kind.
VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER It is not. Ask me how I know.
? PLACEHOLDER "How do you know?"
    set: veteran_asked
    VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER Because I am still here, and they are not.
? PLACEHOLDER Walk away.
    set: veteran_snubbed

== asked
requires: veteran.introduction
when: veteran_asked
act: 1

VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER You asked me a question, last time. Nobody here asks anything. I will remember that.

== snubbed
requires: veteran.introduction
when: veteran_snubbed
act: 1

VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER You walked off on me last time. Fine. The sand will teach you manners.

== first_win
requires: veteran.introduction
when: wins >= 1
act: 1

VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER Heard them from the yard. That noise was for you? Hm. Enjoy it while it lasts.

== many_wins
requires: veteran.first_win
when: wins >= 2

VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER Out through the Triumphalis {wins} times now. I counted mine, once. Then I stopped.

== after_a_fall
requires: veteran.introduction
unless: veteran.first_win
when: last_outcome == fall
act: 1

VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER I watched them carry you off. You held on longer than most.
[last_band == boo] VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER The crowd jeered while they did it. They jeer at the dead too.

== grumble
priority: filler
repeat

VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER Sand in my boots, sand in my bread, sand in my sleep.

== still_here
priority: filler
repeat

VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER Go on. I am not going anywhere.
