== arrival
trigger: enter ludus
priority: story
act: 1

LANISTA: PLACEHOLDER Back through the gate on your own feet. Not all of mine manage that.

== introduction
priority: high
act: 1

LANISTA: PLACEHOLDER This house paid little for you. Show me I robbed the man who sold you.
LANISTA: PLACEHOLDER Every notch in that post was cut by someone like you. Most of them are under the sand now.

== first_win
requires: lanista.introduction
when: wins >= 1
act: 1

LANISTA: PLACEHOLDER Out through the Triumphalis. The emperor smiled, they tell me. I did not see it. I was counting.
[last_outcome == fall] LANISTA: PLACEHOLDER Last night was less pretty. They forget, if you let them.

== after_a_fall
requires: lanista.introduction
unless: lanista.first_win
when: last_outcome == fall
act: 1

LANISTA: PLACEHOLDER They carried you back on a plank. The plank cost me too.
