# The lanista's pool: the owner of the house, who keeps the post in the Ludus. Every line is a
# PLACEHOLDER for the human writing. A merchant has no bark: with nothing new, the post opens its
# panel at once.

# The first return: one beat as the black lifts on the Ludus.
== arrival
trigger: enter ludus
priority: story
act: 1

LANISTA: PLACEHOLDER Still breathing. Not all of mine manage that.

== introduction
priority: high
act: 1

LANISTA: PLACEHOLDER You belong to this house now. See that you are worth your keep.
LANISTA: PLACEHOLDER Every notch in that post was cut by someone like you. Most of them are under the sand now.

== first_win
requires: lanista.introduction
when: wins >= 1
act: 1

LANISTA: PLACEHOLDER A win, they tell me, and the crowd on its feet. I did not see it. I was counting.
[last_outcome == fall] LANISTA: PLACEHOLDER Last night was less pretty. They forget, if you let them.

== after_a_fall
requires: lanista.introduction
unless: lanista.first_win
when: last_outcome == fall
act: 1

LANISTA: PLACEHOLDER They carried you back on a plank. The plank cost me too.
