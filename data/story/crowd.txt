# The crowd's pool: its one-line judgement over the heading when the picker opens after a
# round, from the round's band at its end (round_band) and where the round's favour mostly went
# (round_loss: hit, fled, slow, or none). Every line is a PLACEHOLDER for the human writing. A
# timed event shows only its first shown line, so each event is one line. A pick comes after
# every round but the last, so every event repeats. At the two low bands a judgement of the loss
# outranks the plain line of its band (filler), which speaks when nothing was lost; the two high
# bands praise whatever was lost. Events of one case rotate (the least recently played first).

== boo_hit
when: round_band == boo and round_loss == hit
repeat
trigger: pick

CROWD: PLACEHOLDER The crowd jeers. It thinks you're a clumsy oaf.

== boo_fled
when: round_band == boo and round_loss == fled
repeat
trigger: pick

CROWD: PLACEHOLDER The crowd spits and cries coward.

== boo_slow
when: round_band == boo and round_loss == slow
repeat
trigger: pick

CROWD: PLACEHOLDER The crowd howls. It thinks you're far too slow.

== boo
when: round_band == boo
priority: filler
repeat
trigger: pick

CROWD: PLACEHOLDER The crowd sits in cold silence.

== quiet_hit
when: round_band == quiet and round_loss == hit
repeat
trigger: pick

CROWD: PLACEHOLDER The crowd winces. It thinks you're clumsy.

== quiet_fled
when: round_band == quiet and round_loss == fled
repeat
trigger: pick

CROWD: PLACEHOLDER The crowd murmurs. It thinks you're a coward.

== quiet_slow
when: round_band == quiet and round_loss == slow
repeat
trigger: pick

CROWD: PLACEHOLDER The crowd is restless. It thinks you're slow.

== quiet

priority: filler
