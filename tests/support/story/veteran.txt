# The veteran's pool in the fixture story: a first word with lines and a choice (one choice's
# own line reads the flag it sets), a word that waits on it, a word gated on the lanista's, a
# choice per flag, and a bark.

# The first word: the box suites' event (two lines, then two choices).
== hello

VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER New blood, and still standing.
VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER The sand remembers the slow.
? PLACEHOLDER Ask his name.
    set: veteran_trust
    [veteran_trust] VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER Names are for the dead.
? PLACEHOLDER Walk on.
    set: veteran_distant

== next_night
requires: veteran.hello

VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER Another night, then.

== the_warning
requires: lanista.first_word
unless: veteran.the_goodbye
when: deaths >= 1 and not veteran_distant
priority: high
repeat
act: 2

VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER {deaths} falls, and you still walk.
[last_verdict == down] VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER I saw them carry you out.
? PLACEHOLDER Say nothing.
    set: veteran_distant
? PLACEHOLDER "So did you."
    set: veteran_trust
    VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER Hm.
set: veteran_met

== the_goodbye
requires: veteran.the_warning
when: veteran_trust or (runs > 3 and last_killer == boss)
act: 3

VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER Go well.

== grumble
priority: filler
repeat

VETERAN: PLACEHOLDER Sand in everything.
