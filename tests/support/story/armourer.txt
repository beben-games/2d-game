# The armourer's pool in the fixture story: lines after a choice group read the flags the choice
# set (a gated line, a substitution), and a second choice group after them.

== two_asks

ARMOURER: PLACEHOLDER Steel or leather?
? PLACEHOLDER Steel.
    set: armourer_steel
    set: lanista_count = 3
? PLACEHOLDER Leather.
[armourer_steel] ARMOURER: PLACEHOLDER Steel it is, then.
ARMOURER: PLACEHOLDER {lanista_count} blades on the rack.
? PLACEHOLDER Ask the lanista's mood.
    set: lanista_mood = pleased
? PLACEHOLDER Say nothing.
[lanista_mood == pleased] ARMOURER: PLACEHOLDER Pleased, for once.
